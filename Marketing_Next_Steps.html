6Channel guardrails (quick reference)

    

      
ChannelDoDon't

      
        

          Google Search
          LegitScript-cert first, exact + phrase match, conservative budget, generic conversion events.
          Remarketing, Customer Match, audience segments built from quiz data, "generic Ozempic" claims.
        

        

          Meta / Instagram
          Education content, process explainers, 18+ only, served-state geo only.
          Before/after imagery, body-shame hooks, "you" copy that implies obesity/disease, retargeting from health intake.
        

        

          TikTok
          Organic + Spark testing first, verified medical voices, "what to ask before starting GLP-1 care" themes.
          Drug-name-heavy direct-response ads pre-approval; transformation/challenge montages.
        

        

          Affiliate / creator
          Manual approval, required disclaimer copy, FTC disclosure language, post-monitoring.
          Fake/AI doctors, borrowed credentials, drug-equivalency claims (MEDVi-pattern).
