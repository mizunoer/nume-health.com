# LegitScript Meeting Brief — 2026-07-15

_Prep for the LegitScript intro call. Two properties: nume-health.com (DTC cash-pay GLP-1
telehealth; marketing brand + independent clinicians) and mythic-rx.com (503A compounding
pharmacy, B2B partner-physician site)._

## Verified numbers (legitscript.com, checked 2026-07-15)

- Application fee: **$975 per website, nonrefundable**
- Annual certification fee: **$2,150 per website** (starts only once certified)
- Expedited review: **+$2,500** per application — review starts within 2 business days
- Probationary tier (past compliance issues): **$3,995/yr** — avoid
- Both domains ≈ **$6,250 year one**; nume alone ≈ **$3,125** then $2,150/yr
- Review: weeks to a few months; Google/Meta run their own certification after (~2–4 wks)
- Context: FDA warning letters to ~30 GLP-1 telehealth companies (Mar 2026); Google
  recurring-billing policy for Rx effective 2026-03-31

## Questions (priority order)

**Certifiability**
1. Will you certify cash-pay telehealth offering compounded semaglutide/tirzepatide from a
   503A post-shortage? What documentation that compounds aren't "essentially copies"
   (personalization rationale)? Any dose forms/combos you won't certify (B12 blends,
   sublingual)?
2. Entity structure: marketing brand + friendly-PC/MSO clinicians + separate 503A. Who is
   the applicant? One cert covering both sites, or two?
3. Does mythic-rx.com need cert at all today (B2B, no online consumer sales)? Is the
   trigger card-not-present payments / consumer ads?

**Process & cost**
4. Confirm fees + expedite; any other fees (re-review, adding a domain, monitoring)?
   Multi-site discount?
5. Current review queue for GLP-1 telehealth; top 3 deficiencies that stall applications
   like ours?
6. If denied: is the $975 lost, reapplication path/cooldown, pre-application gap review
   offered?
7. What lands an applicant in probationary ($3,995/yr) vs standard?

**What it unlocks**
8. Which platform programs does cert satisfy (Google healthcare cert, Meta, TikTok,
   Microsoft)? Impact of Google's Rx recurring-billing policy on cash-pay subscriptions?
9. **Even certified, will Google/Meta actually run ads for compounded GLP-1s, or do
   platform policies block them regardless?** (If blocked → cert buys processing + trust,
   not ads; changes ROI.)
10. Which processors recognize cert for GLP-1 telehealth; does it reduce reserves?

**Ongoing**
11. Monitoring obligations; revocation triggers + cure process; what changes force
    re-review (new meds, new states, redesign, new domain)?
12. Any states you require us to exclude (async-prescribing restrictions)?

## Leave the meeting with

Itemized written quote (per domain, ± expedite) · document checklist for our exact model ·
citable answer on compounded-GLP-1 certifiability · applicant-entity guidance for MSO
structure · realistic timeline · portal/sample application access · named account contact.

## Our prep list (what they'll need from us)

- Corporate: entity docs/EIN, ownership, DBA linking Nume brand → entity, MSO/medical-group
  agreements
- Clinical: clinician roster + state licenses, prescribing protocol (sync/async per state),
  ID verification, patient-prescriber relationship documentation
- Pharmacy (Mythic): state license + non-resident ship-to licenses, 503A registration, API
  sourcing docs
- Website: HIPAA privacy policy (must state mobile opt-in data never sold — ties to A2P),
  ToS, telehealth consent, refund policy, transparent pricing, med risk disclosures.
  **GAP: nume's four legal pages don't exist yet — hard blocker, drafting in progress.**
- Marketing: claims inventory; no "FDA-approved" language for compounded products (the FDA
  warning-letter trigger)

## Alternatives if too expensive

| Path | Cost | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LegitScript, nume only (floor) | ~$3,125 yr 1 | ~1–3 mo + platform certs | Defer mythic entirely |
| NABP Digital Pharmacy (mythic, later) | Quote; typically higher, on-site survey | 3–6+ mo | Google accepts for pharmacy ads; stronger 503A credential; overkill for B2B today |
| No cert — organic/B2B only | $0 | now | SEO/email/partner outreach fine; no paid DTC ads; processing harder under 2026 card rules |
| High-risk processor, no cert | 3.5–5% + reserves | 1–2 wk | Payments bridge only; tightening; no ads |

**Framing:** mythic (B2B) can skip LegitScript today. For nume (DTC GLP-1 paid acquisition)
there is no substitute — Google and Meta both gate on it. Decision hinges on Q9: if
compounded GLP-1 ads are blocked regardless, defer cert until payments force it and grow
nume organically.
