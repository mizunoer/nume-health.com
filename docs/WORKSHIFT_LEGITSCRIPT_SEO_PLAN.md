# Will natural SEO + low-spend campaigns work? — LegitScript concept assessment

_2026-08-10. Companion to LEGITSCRIPT_MEETING_BRIEF.md and LEGITSCRIPT_COMPLIANCE.md.
Internal — contains negotiated program numbers; do not publish or link from any site._

## The short answer

**Yes for workshift.io, yes-but-slowly for Nume organic, and paid DTC GLP-1 stays
gated regardless of spend level.** These are three different battles that the ad
platforms treat completely differently:

| Property | Battle | Gated by LegitScript? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| workshift.io | B2B SaaS/platform marketing | **No** — it's software marketing, not healthcare services | Natural SEO + low-spend works now; best ROI of the three |
| nume-health.com organic | DTC healthcare content | No (organic is never gated) | Works, but it's YMYL — slow build, needs clinician-authored content |
| nume-health.com paid | DTC GLP-1 ads | **Yes — hard gate** | No spend level fixes this before certification; low-spend ≠ allowed spend |

The strategic insight from the meeting brief (Q9) still governs: even *with*
certification, whether Google/Meta actually serve compounded-GLP-1 ads is
platform policy, not certification. Until that's answered in writing,
**organic is the mainline for Nume, not the fallback** — which makes the natural
SEO investment the safe bet either way.

## 1. workshift.io — the LegitScript partner concept (best case for natural SEO)

Nobody gates B2B platform keywords. The searcher is a founder/marketer at a
healthcare startup discovering that their site fails certification — high intent,
low competition, and the content practically writes itself from our own docs.

**Keyword clusters (long-tail, low competition, high intent):**
- "legitscript certification requirements / cost / how long" — informational, top of funnel
- "legitscript certification checklist telehealth" / "website requirements"
- "legitscript application denied / stalled" — pain, mid-funnel
- "healthcare website compliance platform" / "HIPAA compliant marketing website"
- "A2P 10DLC healthcare SMS consent" — adjacent cluster we already have authority-grade content for
- "google ads healthcare certification" / "meta healthcare advertiser"

**Content roadmap — one page per certification standard.** The 9 standards in
LEGITSCRIPT_COMPLIANCE.md are 9 evergreen guides (licensure disclosure, legal-page
set, transparent pricing, outbound-link discipline, seal usage, 30-day reporting…).
Each ends with the same CTA → /legitscript-partners.html. This is exactly the
content Google rewards in a niche: specific, experienced, unavailable elsewhere.
The FAQPage schema on the partner page is already live for rich results.

**Low-spend paid that works here (est. $300–800/mo total):**
- Branded search (defensive, pennies).
- Exact-match long-tail Google Search on the clusters above — B2B software terms,
  no healthcare-ads policy in the way. Start $10–20/day, kill anything without
  demo/email conversions in 30 days.
- Microsoft Ads clone of the winners (~30–50% cheaper clicks, B2B skew).
- LinkedIn: organic founder-posts first; paid only for retargeting site visitors
  later. Cold LinkedIn ads are NOT low-spend — skip.

**Expectation:** niche B2B long-tail can rank in weeks-to-months, not the 6–12mo
of competitive SEO. The partner-program page + 3–4 standards guides is a credible
v1 that can start compounding immediately.

## 2. nume-health.com — organic (works, with discipline)

- **YMYL reality:** health content ranks only with real E-E-A-T — clinician
  bylines (Nico/Sara), reviewed-by lines, citations, and the legal pages live.
  The pre-submission checklist items (privacy, ToS, consent, refund, service-states)
  are also SEO trust signals — one workstream, two payoffs.
- **What to write:** cash-pay/pricing transparency content ("GLP-1 without
  insurance", "compounded semaglutide cost"), how-telehealth-works, state-by-state
  availability pages that mirror the licensure list (never ahead of it — standard 1
  requires the match, and the service-state pages double as local-intent SEO).
- **What NOT to do:** no "FDA-approved" language for compounds (FDA warning-letter
  trigger, standard 9), no outbound links to uncertified healthcare sites
  (including mythic-rx.com until certified), no claims the landing factory hasn't
  inventoried. Organic content is crawled during certification review — the site's
  content IS part of the application.
- **Expectation:** 6–12 months to meaningful non-brand traffic. Brand search +
  referral/partner traffic carries the interim.

## 3. nume-health.com — paid (the honest part)

- Google/Meta DTC ads for compounded GLP-1: **blocked pre-certification**, and
  possibly restricted post-certification (Q9 — still unanswered in writing; get
  Tyler's compliance contact to answer it before modeling ad-driven CAC).
- "Low spend" doesn't route around a policy gate — it just loses less money on
  disapproved/limited ads. Don't test-spend against the gate.
- What IS available pre-cert: branded search (usually approvable — it's the
  brand, not the drug), and non-drug creative angles are still healthcare-services
  ads and will hit the same certification wall on Google/Meta. Treat paid DTC as
  **post-certification work**, sequenced: certify → platform healthcare-advertiser
  programs (~2–4 wks) → then low-spend testing.
- Card rails note: certification also improves processing standing — the payments
  benefit is real even if ads stay restricted (this was the fallback framing in
  the meeting brief).

## 4. How the partner program feeds SEO (the flywheel)

Each certified partner = a certified-site launch story → a co-branded customer
spotlight (Tyler offered this explicitly — LegitScript's own marketing linking to
us is the single highest-authority backlink available in this niche) → a case
study page on workshift.io → more "legitscript certification help" rankings →
more partners into the referral block. The 12-month floating-code mechanics
(reclaim + reseed a new partner yearly) mean the content flywheel and the code
block amortize together.

**Timing flag:** Tyler's exception pricing ($400 app / $1,850 code / free expedite
on the first two) has a **mid-August commit deadline**. The SEO plan doesn't
depend on it, but the partner-page pitch ("priority handling") does assume the
program exists.

## 5. Measurement (keep it cheap)

- Google Search Console on workshift.io + nume from day one (free, and the only
  source of truth for what's actually ranking).
- One conversion each: workshift.io = mailto/demo click; nume = funnel entry.
- Monthly 30-minute review: kill paid terms with no conversions, promote content
  that's getting impressions to internal-link targets.
- No SEO tooling spend until GSC shows real impressions to optimize.

## Bottom line

Fund the workshift.io content + partner page now (it's ungated and cheap), build
Nume's organic layer as the certification prerequisites get finished anyway, and
hold all non-brand DTC paid spend until certification lands AND Q9 is answered in
writing. Total paid budget to prove the thesis: **under $1k/month**, most of it
on workshift.io B2B terms.
