# Nume Health Brand Kit

This package breaks the provided composite image into practical logo assets for websites, email signatures, social media, app icons, and marketing material.

## Important limitation
These assets were extracted from the raster PNG you provided. They are usable for web and everyday marketing, but they are not true vector originals. For print/signage or exact brand reproduction, rebuild or export the logo from the original SVG/AI/Figma source.

## Recommended file usage

### Website
- `web/website-header-logo-480w.png` — standard website header/nav logo.
- `web/website-header-logo-320w.png` — smaller mobile header logo.
- `web/favicon.ico` — browser favicon.
- `web/favicon-16x16.png`, `web/favicon-32x32.png`, `web/favicon-48x48.png` — PNG favicon fallbacks.
- `web/apple-touch-icon.png` — iOS home screen icon.
- `web/android-chrome-192x192.png` and `web/android-chrome-512x512.png` — PWA/Android icons.
- `web/site.webmanifest` — starter web app manifest.
- `web/nume-brand.css` — starter CSS variables.

### Email signatures
- `email/email-signature-logo-300w.png` — most email signatures.
- `email/email-signature-logo-600w.png` — high-DPI/retina email signature.

### Social media
- `social/profile-logo-white-1080.png` — profile/avatar on white background.
- `social/profile-logo-teal-1080.png` — profile/avatar on teal background.
- `social/profile-logo-reversed-navy-1080.png` — profile/avatar on dark navy background.
- `social/social-cover-1200x630-reversed.png` — general social sharing/open graph image.
- `social/social-cover-1200x630-white.png` — alternate social sharing image.
- `social/nume-health-social-banner-logo.png` — extracted horizontal social banner logo.

### Marketing / presentations
- `marketing/marketing-primary-transparent.png` — primary color logo on transparent background.
- `marketing/marketing-reversed-on-navy-1200w.png` — reversed logo on navy background.
- `marketing/marketing-social-banner-1200w.png` — wide banner version.

## Practical rules
Use the full horizontal color logo on white or transparent/light backgrounds. Use the reversed white logo on dark navy or dark photo backgrounds. Use the mark-only versions for favicon, app icons, social avatars, and tight spaces. Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the leaf mark.
